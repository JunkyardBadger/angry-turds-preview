## Sound effects
All four samples are **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** from freesound.org. The licence was checked on each sound's page on 2026-10-29. Files in `src/sfx/` were trimmed to a short clip from the freesound preview, faded out, loudness-normalised and saved as mono 64 kbps MP3. No attribution is required, but here it is:

| File | Used for | Source | Author | Licence |

| `src/sfx/plop.mp3` | turd hits something | "Plop!" https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/447910/ | Breviceps | CC0 1.0 |

| `src/sfx/thwop.mp3` | plunger knocked out | "Plunger Pop" https://freesound.org/people/ralph.whitehead/sounds/342815/ | ralph.whitehead | CC0 1.0 |

All other sounds (flap, explode, glass, coin, UI clicks) are synthesized in `src/sound.ts`.
